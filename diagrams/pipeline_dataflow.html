Pipeline Notes

ROI crop (S2) precedes motion gating (S3): MOG2 runs exclusively on the cropped sub-frame at 320×320, keeping the background model stable and lane calibration valid.

S4 (FEP + Tiling) groups the two optional pre-processing paths in one card. FEP corrects fisheye distortion (--fep); Tiling splits the crop into a 2×N grid (--force-tiles). Both are independent; neither is required for flat-camera setups.

S6 (Lane Detection) shows the accumulated lane mask state at the point YOLO detections are available. Only vehicle-confirmed frames (S7 → notify_vehicle_detections → S3) contribute to the accumulator, preventing tree/shadow contamination.

S9 (Hazard Detection) checks per-track bboxes against the lane polygon. A dwell counter increments each frame a track overlaps the hazard zone; threshold crossing emits an obstacle_in_lane or slow_vehicle event.

S10 (Output) emits the CBOR MQTT message and MJPEG preview frame. Annotated video is appended to the event .mp4 when recording is enabled.
